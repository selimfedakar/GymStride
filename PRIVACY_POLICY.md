# Privacy Policy — GymStride

**Effective Date:** May 11, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 11, 2026

GymStride is developed and operated by Selim Fedakar. This Privacy Policy describes what data the GymStride iOS application ("App") collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account information:** Email address, full name, and username at registration.
- **Profile information:** Profile photo, fitness goals, workout preferences, and gym location (city or neighborhood — not your precise address).
- **Messages:** Content of chat requests and direct messages exchanged with other users.
- **Workout logs:** Workout type, distance (for runs), optional notes, and timestamps you voluntarily enter.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Approximate location:** Used only to show nearby workout partners. We request foreground permission only — the App never accesses your location in the background.
- **Push notification token:** A device token used to deliver in-app notifications (new messages, chat requests). Not linked to advertising.
- **Device metadata:** Device type and OS version, collected by our infrastructure for error diagnostics.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Display your profile to other authenticated GymStride users searching for partners in your area.
- Enable real-time 1:1 messaging between matched users.
- Send push notifications for new messages and chat requests.
- Evaluate badge eligibility from your logged workout history.
- Diagnose technical issues and improve App performance.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your data for advertising.

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

### With Other Users

Your profile (name, username, profile photo, fitness preferences, general location) is visible to other authenticated users. Direct messages are visible only to you and your conversation partner.

### With Service Providers

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Database, authentication, real-time messaging |
| **Apple Push Notification Service (APNs)** | Push notifications on iOS |
| **Expo / EAS** | App build and deployment infrastructure |

These providers process data only as required to deliver their services and are bound by their own privacy policies.

---

## 4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. On account deletion, your profile, messages, and workout logs are permanently removed from our database within 30 days. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

---

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

GymStride is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has registered, please contact us and we will delete their data promptly.

---

## 7. Security

Data is transmitted over TLS. The database enforces row-level security policies so users can only access records they are authorized to read. Tokens are stored in encrypted device storage. No transmission method is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted. The "Last Updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
